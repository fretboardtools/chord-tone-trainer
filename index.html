12-bar blues in A

Tap a chord to see its tones.

Stay in one area

Pick a five-fret area and keep your hand there. The point is to find the next chord's notes without jumping around the neck.

Aim at the change

The dots switch to the next chord half a beat before it arrives, so you can see where you're going in time to get there. Make it a full beat early if you need more warning.

Then stop looking

Once a progression feels familiar, switch the dots to On the change, then try it with your eyes closed. The dots are there to get you started.
